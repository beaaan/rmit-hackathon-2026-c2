# UNLOCKED: the brief

Summary of Tide's challenge for the RMIT hackathon (Challenge 2). Source: https://tide.org/unlocked.
The page and the organisers are the source of truth if anything here differs.

## The challenge

Build a **small working prototype with TideCloak integrated** that shows one possibility currently
held back by a trust, privacy or security trade-off. In the app, **no single administrator,
organisation or AI can unilaterally override the rules protecting people's data and decisions.**
Demonstrate one new possibility and explain why removing unilateral control makes it possible.

- Use synthetic data and mocked systems.
- No cybersecurity or cryptography experience needed.
- Build with Tide's MCP server in a coding agent, or start from the TideCloak Quickstart.
- Avoid: a project that's mainly a login demo, a security tool or a generic access-control dashboard.
- "If your app would work exactly the same with ordinary login and backend permissions, develop the
  idea further."

## Deliverables

1. **Public GitHub repository** with the working prototype (TideCloak integrated) and the demo video.
2. **README** starting with the official Project overview template: one-sentence description, then
   answers to the five judging prompts, **300 words or fewer**. Setup instructions in a separate section.
3. **Two-minute demo video**, `.mp4`, under 100 MB, saved in the repo. Suggested structure: the
   possibility → the constraint → the prototype and TideCloak's role → what changes.
4. **Submit the repo link** via the Google Form: https://forms.gle/HRHYjnqB86j1LFS97

## Judging criteria

| Weight | Criterion | README prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 25% | Vision & originality | What new product, service, experience or way of working are you proposing? What makes your idea distinctive? |
| 20% | Trust constraint insight | What is difficult, risky or impossible today because trust, privacy or security gets in the way? Explain what becomes possible when that constraint is removed. |
| 20% | Value & impact | Who would benefit, and how would their lives or work improve? Give a concrete example of why this matters. |
| 20% | Why TideCloak matters | How does your application use TideCloak, and how does that integration help enable your idea? |
| 15% | Prototype & storytelling | What does your prototype demonstrate today? Describe the key interaction judges should look for. |
